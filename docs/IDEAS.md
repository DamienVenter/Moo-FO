# MOO-FO — Roadmap & Idea Vault

The user's own future list, plus everything else that would fit the game.

## From the original brief
- Full menu suite; customisation for UFO, beam, and more
- More map unlocks
- Different game modes
- An iconic, original soundtrack
- Multiplayer with friends
- Full time-of-day cycle (night / day / sunset / sunrise, each with unique features)
- Shaders

## Customisation & progression
- **UFO skins**: rusty saucer, stealth black, golden flagship, retro chrome, cardboard-box "budget UFO"
- **Beam colors/styles**: rainbow beam, lightning beam, bubble beam (cows float in bubbles), disco beam
- **Trails**: warp trail cosmetics (star dust, rainbow, smoke rings)
- **Hangar screen**: rotate/inspect your UFO between runs
- **Currency**: "Moolah" earned per run → spend on cosmetics; golden cows pay extra Moolah
- **Daily challenges** ("abduct 5 chickens in one combo") and login streaks
- **Achievements**: "Cattle Rustler" (100 lifetime cows), "Untouchable" (full run, no damage), "Chicken Run" (only chickens)
- **Stats page**: lifetime abductions, accuracy dodged shots, best combo

## Game modes
- **Classic**: the current 90-second dash
- **Endless**: no timer, escalating farmers, one life — leaderboard chase
- **Stampede**: cows constantly sprint across the map; grab them on the move
- **Heist**: one prize bull guarded by every farmer; extraction mission
- **Pacifist**: no health — one hit ends the run; double points
- **Zen ranch**: no farmers, no timer, lo-fi music, just vibes and abductions
- **Boss night**: the army shows up — tank with a searchlight; dodge the spotlight
- **Co-op (2P)**: one flies, one aims a turret-beam; or two UFOs splitting the field
- **Versus**: same field, two UFOs, steal each other's lifted cows

## Maps & world
- **New maps**: desert ranch (tumbleweeds, cacti, vultures), snowy alpine farm (frozen pond = slippery cows), rice paddies, English countryside (hedgerows + castle), Area 51 (turn the tables)
- **Weather**: rain (farmers shelter indoors = free farming), fog (minimap becomes vital), thunderstorms (lightning briefly lights the whole map), wind (affects flight drift)
- **Seasons**: autumn leaves, winter snow blankets, spring blossoms on the orchard
- **Living world**: owls on fence posts, bats around the barn, fireflies near the river, a train crossing the map on a schedule, hot-air balloon drifting by
- **Interactive props**: knock over the outhouse, beam the scarecrow for a decoy bonus, power lines you must fly over or duck under

## Mechanics & spice
- **Power-ups dropped by golden cows**: shield bubble, magnet beam (wider radius), time freeze (+5 s), decoy hologram UFO
- **Cow varieties**: sleeping cows (slow but worth more), bulls (charge and ram fences, worth 3×), calves (fast, travel in pairs — grab both for bonus), the **mythical disco cow** (1/100 runs, 2000 pts, plays its own theme)
- **Farmer escalation**: farmer's wife with a frying pan (melee, fast), sniper on the silo, pickup truck patrol with a searchlight, farm dog that barks and wakes farmers
- **Risk/reward**: cows inside barns worth double — but you must hover low through the open roof hatch
- **Mothership meter**: fill it to call a 5-second mega-beam that vacuums everything in a huge radius
- **Near-miss bonus**: points for grazing past bullets at warp
- **Physics fun**: dropped cows bounce off trampolines (hay piles), beam a hay bale and drop it on a farmer to stun him

## Presentation
- **Photo mode** with filters, free camera, and "alien tourist selfie" frames
- **Replay/kill-cam**: final-cow slow-mo replay at round end
- **Dynamic music** layers: drums fade in when chased, sparkle layer while beaming
- **Announcer voice** (deadpan alien): "BOVINE ACQUIRED."
- **Shader pass**: outline/toon edges, bloom on emissives, heat-haze under the beam, film grain for night
- **Haptics** on mobile (beam rumble, hit thud)
- **Screen-reader friendly menus + colorblind-safe minimap palette**

## Tech & meta
- PWA install (offline play, home-screen icon)
- Cloud saves + global/friend leaderboards
- Seeded daily run (same cow layout for everyone, one attempt)
- Gamepad support
- Level editor: place fences/barns/cows, share via URL seed
- Twitch mode: chat votes to spawn farmers or golden cows
